SHARE-seq campaign with extendable final FRAMES states, 2026-10-18

User request
Relaunch SHARE-seq with resumable checkpoints. The earlier submission already
resumed interrupted allocations, but deleted final checkpoints on completion.
This replacement retains the three final FRAMES confirmation checkpoints so
the anytime schedules can later continue beyond 20,000 updates.

Scientific design unchanged
Exact archived 31,890-cell SHARE-seq matrices/marginals, FP64 arithmetic,
product initialization, and 1,024 fresh iid coupling pairs per stochastic
update. Full-gradient POT: one 2,000-update run. Pair-sampled POT: three
2,000-update runs. POT epsilon=.001; both inner criteria stay at1e-7.
FRAMES:24 prefactor candidates times two development seeds, each20,000 updates,
then the selected candidate on three fresh confirmation seeds811,812,813.
gamma0={.3,1}; rho0={.1,.3,1}; mu_scale={.1,1,10,100}; exponents3/4,1/2,1/4.
This is55 runs total. No random-sign oracle and no change to numerical updates.

Retention and extension
All unfinished tasks retain transactional checkpoints across allocations.
Each final FRAMES confirmation retains raw coupling P, stochastic momentum D,
global update count, per-rank random states, counters and diagnostics. Finished
development and POT tasks retain their metadata but prune tensor checkpoints.
The three confirmation states are the final reported FRAMES replicates.

New runner supports --extend-from CHECKPOINT_DIR into a fresh output directory
with a strictly larger target horizon. Target horizon is separate from the
numerical identity; all other numerical settings, source, data and topology
must match. Same-output interruption resumes remain strict. Parent checkpoints
remain unchanged, including their manifest and provenance. A continuation uses
the original global iteration and momentum; schedules are not restarted.
This launch still targets20,000 FRAMES updates. It enables later extensions;
it does not independently authorize or submit a30,000-update experiment.

Execution and storage
One serial SHARE stream on4A100,8CPUs,128GiB,fwllm/gpua100;24h allocation limit,
22h soft stop, checkpoint every5,000 updates or30min, automatic CPU dispatchers.
Three final FRAMES states occupy45.46GiB; the peak while writing the third
transactional checkpoint is60.62GiB. Reserve62GiB for SHARE and3GiB for the

the existing entropy streams by dependency, avoiding overlapping same-dataset
checkpoint demands. No full retained archive of48 development states.

Validation

against uninterrupted6 bit-for-bit for coupling, momentum, random state,
trajectory and counters, and verify the parent checkpoint is unchanged.
Actual-size gates verify initialization, memory and interruption/resume for
all three methods. Production depends on these new source-specific gates.
