Measured wall-clock replay of the fixed BMMC/SNARE comparison, 2026-10-18

The completed large_pot_frames_20261016 experiment recorded exact loss traces
by iteration and total elapsed seconds, but no per-diagnostic timestamps.
Its existing loss trajectories cannot be reconstructed against actual time.
The accompanying immediate figures therefore show measured final endpoints
against total elapsed time and separately show the measured iteration curves.
No interpolation of iteration into estimated time is used.

Replay the eight reported runs only, without any prefactor selection:
BMMC6224: full-gradient entropic GW POT step, seed810,2000updates; pair-sampled
FRAMES gamma0=.3,rho0=1,mu_scale=10,seeds811/812/813,20000updates each.
SNARE10000: the same POT control; FRAMES gamma0=.3,rho0=.1,mu_scale=10,
seeds811/812/813,20000updates each. All use archived matrices and marginals,
FP64, product initialization, epsilon=.001, inner criteria1e-7, and1024pairs
per stochastic update. FRAMES exponents remain3/4,1/2,1/4. Diagnostics retain
the original iteration schedule. Source-specific CPU and actual-size A100
gates precede production; the latter checks the first two loss measurements
against frozen original metadata. Original campaigns and outputs are unchanged.

One serial stream of eight runs uses1A100,2CPUs,32GiB,fwllm/gpua100.6-hour
allocations have5-hour soft stops and checkpoint-driven continuations.
Only unfinished task tensor checkpoints persist; all loss/timing metadata and
terminal receipts persist. The historical run totals suggest roughly four
GPU-hours for the eight replays, before gates/setup; this is not a guarantee.
There is no active monitoring or repeated scheduler polling.

Storage: one paused replay may retain at most one FP64 SNARE P,D state,
1.490GiB. While SHARE uses4A100s, Ruche's four-A100 user limit excludes an
active replay. SHARE transactional peak60.616GiB + two entropy streams2.067GiB
+ paused replay1.490GiB =64.174GiB, within the existing65GiB reservation.
While the replay is active, SHARE can retain at most three completed/paused
states45.462GiB, so the replay's two-generation3GiB transient remains below
that bound. Finished replay states must be pruned idempotently.

Timing counts elapsed execution, including load/validation, diagnostics and
checkpoint overhead. Queue delays and the48-run development sweep are excluded.
Comparisons of final fixed-horizon times are not time-to-equal-loss comparisons;
POT may reach its final plateau before the prescribed2000updates finish.
