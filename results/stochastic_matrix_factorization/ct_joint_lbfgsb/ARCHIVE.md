# Joint L-BFGS-B CT evidence

This archive preserves 12 development runs selecting L-BFGS-B memory and 28 deterministic evaluation runs without spectral constraints, including every accepted joint update and terminal charged-work state. All solver forward/adjoint calls, including rejected line-search trials, are charged. Previous comparisons remain in the separate ct and ct_nonnegative archives.

Files retain runtime-relative paths and original bytes. Embedded absolute paths are provenance. The manifest records sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Raw CT and calibration data, optimizer checkpoints, environments, fixtures, caches and transient logs remain outside Git.

See the [paper bundle](../../../paper/experiments/stochastic_ct/) for the report and public appendix.
